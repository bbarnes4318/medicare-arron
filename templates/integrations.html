📖 Example Guide

Setting Up Ringba Integration

Follow this step-by-step example to connect Ringba (or use it as a template for any other platform)

Step 1

Get Your Ringba Webhook URL

In Ringba:

  1. Log into your Ringba account
  2. Go to Settings → Webhooks
  3. Click "Create New Webhook"
  4. Copy the webhook URL (it will look like: https://api.ringba.com/v2/webhook/...)
💡 Tip: The webhook URL is unique to your account. Keep it safe!
Ringba Dashboard
⚙️ Settings
🔗 Webhooks
📊 Reports
Step 2

Add Integration in Hopwhistle

  1. Click the "+ Create Integration" button below
  2. Name: Enter "Ringba" (or any name you prefer)
  3. Webhook URL: Paste the URL you copied from Ringba
💡 Note: This works for any platform - just use their webhook URL!
Step 3

Map Your Fields

Match our form fields with Ringba's expected field names:

First Name → first_name
Phone Number → caller_id
Email → email_address
✅ Pro Tip: Check their API documentation for exact field names!
