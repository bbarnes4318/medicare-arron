What is this page for?

This page allows you to automatically send every new lead you submit to another system in real-time. For example, you can send leads directly to your CRM (like Salesforce, HubSpot) or a Dialer (like Ringba, Convoso).

How to set it up:

  1. Click the + Add Integration button (top right).
  2. Name it: Give it a name you recognize (e.g., "My CRM").
  3. Destination URL: Paste the "Webhook URL" or "API Endpoint" provided by your other software.
  4. Map Fields: Tell us which field matches theirs. For example, if they call the phone number field caller_id, type caller_id next to our "Phone Number" field.
  5. Click Save. We will now send data to that URL every time you submit a form.
